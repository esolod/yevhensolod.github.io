Why plan the trade on the chart

Most position-size math happens off the chart — a separate calculator, a spreadsheet, or a guess. By the time you've done the math, the setup has moved.

Trade Setup Tool keeps the whole plan on the chart: drag the entry, stop, and target anchors, and the size, dollar risk, and R:R update live next to the drawing. When the setup is right, right-click and submit — the bracket order goes out with stop and target already attached and auto-managed.

Typical use: visually planning a trade before it's live, confirming size and R:R against your own risk rules, then executing without leaving the chart.

FAQ

Does it place real orders, or just draw on the chart?

Both. It's a drawing tool by default — nothing is sent to your account until you right-click and choose "Submit Entry Order." Until then it's just a visual plan.

What happens to the stop and target after entry fills?

They're submitted alongside the entry as a bracket and managed automatically — when one side fills or is cancelled, the other is cancelled with it.

Fixed Contracts or Risk Based — what's the difference?

Fixed Contracts always sends the same size. Risk Based divides your risk-per-trade ($) by the stop distance to size the position for you, recalculated live as you move the stop.

Does it work with Sim and live accounts?

Yes, it works with any connected account, Sim or live, with no additional configuration.
